How your export gets read

  1. ChatGPT

    A record carrying mapping, an object of nodes with parent and children, plus a current_node leaf. ChatVault walks the parent chain from that leaf to get the conversation you actually had, then indexes the other branches too and marks them.

  2. Claude

    A record carrying chat_messages, whose entries have a sender of human or assistant and text either in text or in a content array of typed blocks. Both shapes are read.

  3. Gemini

    Google Takeout activity, as JSON records with a Gemini header and a time, or as the MyActivity HTML page. Takeout does not group turns into conversations, so ChatVault groups them by a 30 minute gap and says so in the reading pane.

  4. Anything else

    If none of the three structures match, you point at the fields yourself. The mapping is stored under a hash of the file's key shape, so the next export of the same format is read without asking.

Getting your export

  1. Open Settings, then Data controls, then Export data.
  2. Confirm. The link arrives by email, usually within a few minutes, occasionally after some hours on large accounts.
  3. The download link expires after 24 hours. Save the zip somewhere before it does.
  4. Drop the whole zip here. ChatVault reads conversations.json out of it without unpacking the rest.

What this page requests from the network

Four hosts, and you can check every one of them in the Network panel while you use the tool. Your conversations are not on this list because there is no endpoint in this page that could send them. Once the page and its fonts are cached, you can go offline and keep searching.

The vault lives in IndexedDB in this browser profile. Removing it removes it: there is no copy anywhere else, which is the trade this design makes on purpose.
